■ THE DANCING DEMON ■
150 bpm

A nod to Leo Christopherson's Dancing Demon (1979) for the TRS-80. This demon is my own drawing in the same block characters, not his.

A first running demo

This is the first running demo. I welcome collaboration: ideas, fixes, pull requests, and above all reports from real Model 4s.

I left out some hacks that don't seem to work on every Model 4. This could be pushed a bit further.

The demo

Eight minutes in trs80gp, set up as an original Model 4, at normal speed with sound. TRSDOS boots, the program loads, and the computer plays E1M1 (press T to let it): it walks, fights, and presses the exit switch more than once.

Screens

What's in it

  • E1M1's layout, its doors and the exit switch
  • 15 of the level's wall textures in four greys, darker where the level is dark
  • Zombiemen, shotgun guys, imps and demons, each with its own pictures, health and attack
  • Health, armour, ammunition, a keycard, barrels, trees and lamps
  • A status bar with a face drawn in block graphics
  • A floor that reflects the walls
  • Sound on the one-bit speaker

Limits

  • One floor height; diagonal walls become steps
  • Monsters always show their front view
  • One kind of ammunition; the fist and the pistol
  • Run so far in trs80gp, not yet on a real Model 4

Run it

./setup.sh    # zmac, trs80gp, and a Freedoom checkout
./run.sh      # TRSDOS 6 boots and runs DOOM/CMD
make test     # the checks

On a real Model 4 with 128 KB, copy DOOM/CMD to a TRSDOS 6 or LS-DOS 6 disk and type DOOM.
